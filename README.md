# Kant & Vedanta · Three Films

Three short narrated animations about one idea: that time, space and causation belong to the mind. Kant argued it in 1781; Swami Vivekananda said India's sages had found it long before, and had gone looking for what lies beyond.

1. **The Wall Called Reason** (`film1/`, 4:40): the idea itself.
2. **The Student Who Read Kant** (`film2/`, 4:20): young Naren in Calcutta.
3. **Kant Comes Home** (`film3/`, 4:46): the journey, from Baranagore to Kiel, London and the 1899 voyage.

Each film is a single HTML page that draws every frame on a canvas, kept in sync with its `narration.mp3`. There is no build step, so GitHub Pages serves the folder as it is.

Sources: the Complete Works of Swami Vivekananda and related biographies at ramakrishnavivekananda.info. They are listed on the home page, and each chapter names its source. Narration is synthesised speech (Kokoro).
