Five ways to shoot it

Each reel below animates the same fourteen seconds of the Colombo lecture in a different direction. Every frame is still drawn live from one timeline, so whichever you pick stays seekable, renders to MP4, and plays on a phone. The marks under each reel are its cuts; the white ones are the narration beats. Narration is one voice from here on.
