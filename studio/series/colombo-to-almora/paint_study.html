Paint style

Faster, and never the same twice

An object takes a fraction of a second: outline, then colour flooding in by sweep, bloom or drop, chosen per object. With twenty to forty objects per scene, the screen is always changing.

Objects from the sentence

Colombo gets the lighthouse, the steamship, outrigger canoes, the welcome arch, bunting, garlands and the crowd. The ploughman gets the oxen, rice fields, huts, a well, the temple tower and women carrying water.
