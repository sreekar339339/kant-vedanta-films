From Colombo to Almora · whiteboard studies

Marker on a whiteboard

Four takes on the hand-drawn whiteboard look you pointed to, each animating the same fourteen seconds of the Colombo lecture with original drawings. The marks under each reel are its cuts and camera moves; the dark ones are the narration beats. Narration stays one voice.

Real marker, real board

The reference channel says all its art is hand-drawn on an actual whiteboard with dry-erase markers. White ground, black line, nothing glossy.

Hatching does the shading

Form comes from line weight and cross-hatching, not gradients. Faces and figures are expressive, a little caricatured, never clip-art.

Colour is a marker, used sparingly

Mostly black and white. One or two saturated marker colours scribbled in for the thing that matters, with bursts of full colour for big moments.

One idea, one drawing

Symbolic props carry the argument: a light bulb, a lamp, a globe. Hand-lettered capitals name the idea.

Each frame is still drawn live from one timeline, so whichever you choose stays seekable, renders to MP4 and plays on a phone. The drawings here are original and only borrow the technique, not any of the reference channel's artwork.
